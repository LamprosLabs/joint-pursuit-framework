# Three-minute QuickByte: Can AI Help Two Companies Write One Honest Proposal?

## Goal

Introduce JPF, show one concrete failure mode, and invite people to test version 0.2.

## Suggested flow

### 0:00–0:30 — The problem

“Two companies collaborate on an RFP. Each has different experience, evidence, pricing authority, and delivery responsibilities. Then AI turns all of their material into one smooth proposal—and smooth language can hide who actually knows, approved, priced, or owns each promise.”

### 0:30–1:00 — The sharp point

“The proposal can be combined. Accountability cannot be blended.”

Show the four claim types:

- One company has the capability.
- One person has relevant experience.
- The partners have defined complementary roles.
- The same partners have proven joint delivery.

“AI should never silently turn the first three into the fourth.”

### 1:00–1:45 — JPF 0.2

Show the repository and explain the minimum method:

1. A pursuit charter before substantive drafting
2. One authoritative register linking requirement, evidence, claim owner, delivery owner, and approval
3. Three gates: charter/data use; requirement/delivery ownership; submission freeze/closeout

### 1:45–2:30 — Tiny demonstration

Use the fictional example:

**Unsafe:** “Our team has proven emergency-response support.”

**Register reveals:** One partner monitors systems, another handles business-hours repairs, and nobody approved 24/7 engineering response.

**Safer:** “Northstar will provide continuous automated monitoring and incident intake. Pine & Signal will provide business-hours application repair. After-hours engineering response is excluded unless separately contracted.”

Explain that AI helped find and rewrite the ambiguity; named humans still approved the commitment.

### 2:30–3:00 — Invitation

“JPF 0.2 is a working draft built from one real joint RFP pilot. I’m looking for teams willing to try the Markdown templates on a small pursuit and tell me where the method prevents mistakes—and where it is just overhead.”

Display the GitHub URL and a QR code after the repository is published.

## Optional audience question

“Where has a joint proposal become ambiguous in your work: evidence, pricing, client communication, or who had to deliver the promise?”

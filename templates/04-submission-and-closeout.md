# JPF Submission and Closeout

## Gate 3: pre-submission assurance

### Requirements and claims

- [ ] Every mandatory requirement has a final disposition.
- [ ] Material claims match their evidence class and approval state.
- [ ] No language implies unproven joint delivery.
- [ ] Assumptions, exclusions, and exceptions are buyer-visible where needed.
- [ ] Meeting notes and AI summaries were verified against authoritative sources.

### Delivery and commercial accountability

- [ ] Every promised outcome has one accountable delivery owner.
- [ ] Owners approved scope, acceptance criteria, dependencies, staffing, and estimates.
- [ ] Pricing reconciles with work packages, third-party costs, support, contingency, and term.
- [ ] Monitoring, intake, escalation, and repair obligations are distinguished.
- [ ] Change control, support, warranty, and transition obligations are explicit.

### Information and approval

- [ ] Every submitted evidence item is permitted for buyer use.
- [ ] Names, résumés, client references, pricing, and restricted details have required approval.
- [ ] Each participating organization approved its representations and commitments.
- [ ] Final legal/procurement review is complete when required.

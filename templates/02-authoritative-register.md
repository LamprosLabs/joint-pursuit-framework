# JPF Authoritative Register

This is the source of truth for pursuit status. Meeting notes, chats, email, source documents, and AI output may propose updates; they do not silently override this register.

## Status vocabulary

- **Draft** — working interpretation, not approved
- **Needs evidence** — claim lacks adequate support
- **Needs owner** — delivery accountability is unresolved
- **Needs approval** — owner/evidence exists but required approval is missing
- **Approved with conditions** — usable only with recorded qualifications
- **Approved** — ready for the stated use
- **Excluded** — deliberately not offered or represented
- **Superseded** — retained for history but no longer current

## Evidence classes

- **Product documentation** — vendor or product description
- **Demonstration** — bounded observed behavior
- **Planned configuration** — capability expected after configuration
- **Custom development** — capability requiring design, implementation, and test
- **Proven implementation** — evidence from comparable real delivery

## Gate 2 review

- [ ] Every material RFP requirement has a source reference and status.
- [ ] Every material claim has an owner and an appropriate evidence class.
- [ ] Company capability, personnel experience, coordinated capability, and proven joint delivery are not conflated.
- [ ] Every promised outcome has one accountable delivery owner.
- [ ] Estimates, exclusions, dependencies, and commercial assumptions are visible.
- [ ] Conflicts and missing approvals are marked rather than hidden in prose.
- [ ] Domain and commercial owners approved the representations attributed to them.

# Fictional Example: Civic Services Portal

This example is fictional and intentionally small. **Northstar Digital** is the prime and experience-design partner. **Pine & Signal Engineering** is the technical subcontractor.

## Charter excerpt

- Northstar owns buyer communication, proposal assembly, research, UX, content, and commercial submission.
- Pine & Signal owns architecture, integrations, implementation, testing, deployment, and application repair.
- Northstar may share approved requirement excerpts and draft questions with Pine & Signal for this opportunity only.
- Neither party may represent the other's pricing, personnel availability, prior delivery, or compliance position without recorded approval.

## What JPF prevented

An early AI draft said:

> Our team has proven municipal SSO experience and provides 24/7 support.

That sentence blended one partner's experience, a buyer-specific dependency, automated monitoring, incident intake, and engineering repair into two stronger promises than the evidence supported.

The register enabled a safer answer because it showed:

- Which company owned the SSO evidence
- What remained buyer-dependent
- Who monitored the service
- Who repaired the application and during which hours
- That the firms had coordinated roles, not proven joint delivery

The corrected language was slightly less fluent and materially more deliverable.

# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-02

- Renamed Joint Sales Framework to Joint Pursuit Framework.
- Reframed JPF as a proposal-governance workflow rather than an agent interoperability protocol.
- Reduced the required process to three approval gates.
- Established one authoritative register as the center of the workflow.
- Moved pursuit charter, commercial structure, and data-use decisions before substantive drafting.
- Required one accountable delivery owner per promised outcome.
- Distinguished product documentation, demonstration, planned configuration, custom development, and proven implementation.
- Added submission freeze, manifest, closeout, retention, and delivery-transition steps.
- Added a portable AI skill, templates, a fictional example, and QuickByte outline.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-16

- Initial Joint Sales Framework working draft.
- Defined sovereignty, permission envelopes, capability attribution, human approval, work-package accountability, and a seven-phase agent-exchange workflow.
